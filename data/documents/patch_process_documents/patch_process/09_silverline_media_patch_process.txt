CSOP - PP - PATCH PROCESS - SILVERLINE MEDIA Environment: LAB SAMPLE
ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

OVERVIEW This procedure defines patching and reboot schedules for
Silverline Media. Production schedules, editing sessions, and rendering
activity must be considered.

SERVER PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Monthly Day: Last Sunday of each month Time: 12:00 AM - 6:00 AM Pacific
Time

Patch Remediation Install approved patches during the maintenance
window. Verify storage and media services after patching.

Reboot Policy Shared storage and production servers require explicit
approval before reboot. Do not reboot a server while an active
production workflow depends on it unless the incident/change owner
authorizes the action.

WORKSTATION PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Sunday Time: 1:00 AM - 5:00 AM Pacific Time

Reboot Policy Standard office workstations may be configured for
automated reboot at 4:00 AM Sunday. Editing, rendering, and studio
workstations must be coordinated with the assigned operator.

PATCHING EXCEPTIONS Live Production and Rendering Devices registered for
live production or long-running rendering jobs must not be rebooted
until the production owner confirms a safe window. Record the exception
owner and expiry.

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS 1. Check the production calendar and active
workload. 2. Confirm device group and maintenance window. 3. Obtain
approval for shared storage and production server reboots. 4. Verify
patch completion and storage/service health. 5. Document failures and
deferred devices. 6. Escalate through the Silverline Media Ticket
Process.

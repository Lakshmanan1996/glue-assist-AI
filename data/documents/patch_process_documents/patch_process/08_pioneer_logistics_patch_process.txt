CSOP - PP - PATCH PROCESS - PIONEER LOGISTICS Environment: LAB SAMPLE
ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

OVERVIEW These instructions define patching and reboot procedures for
Pioneer Logistics warehouses and offices. Warehouse shift schedules must
be checked before maintenance.

SERVER PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Sunday Time: 1:00 AM - 5:00 AM Local Warehouse Time

Patch Remediation Install approved patches during the maintenance
window. Confirm warehouse code and local time zone.

Reboot Policy Warehouse infrastructure servers require customer approval
before reboot. Central dispatch servers must be covered by an approved
change and an agreed operational window.

WORKSTATION PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Sunday Time: 12:00 AM - 6:00 AM Local Warehouse Time

Reboot Policy Office workstations may be configured for automated reboot
at 5:00 AM Sunday. Warehouse handhelds, scanners, and shift-critical
terminals must be coordinated with the warehouse supervisor.

PATCHING EXCEPTIONS Peak Dispatch Period Devices listed on the approved
peak-period exception register must not be rebooted during the stated
dispatch period. Confirm the exception expiry before deferring
maintenance.

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS 1. Confirm warehouse code, shift, and time
zone. 2. Check the peak dispatch exception register. 3. Verify the
approved patch window. 4. Obtain approval for shared server reboots. 5.
Verify patch and service status. 6. Record failed patches and reboot
issues. 7. Escalate through the Pioneer Logistics Ticket Process.

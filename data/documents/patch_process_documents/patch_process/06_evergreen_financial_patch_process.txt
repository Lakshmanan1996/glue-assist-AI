CSOP - PP - PATCH PROCESS - EVERGREEN FINANCIAL Environment: LAB SAMPLE
ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

OVERVIEW This document defines patching and reboot controls for
Evergreen Financial. All production patching must be linked to an
approved change record.

SERVER PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Monthly Day: Second Saturday of each month Time: 10:00 PM - 3:00 AM
Eastern Time

Patch Remediation Install only updates included in the approved change
scope. Record patch identifiers, results, and any failed installations.

Reboot Policy Production servers require explicit written approval and a
valid change reference before reboot. No response or lack of objection
must not be treated as approval.

WORKSTATION PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Sunday Time: 12:00 AM - 4:00 AM Eastern Time

Reboot Policy Standard workstations may be configured for automated
reboot at 3:00 AM Sunday, subject to the approved endpoint policy.
Privileged administrator workstations require coordination with the
customer IT team.

PATCHING EXCEPTIONS Transaction and Core Banking Systems These systems
must not be patched or rebooted under the general schedule. A
system-owner-approved change is required, including validation and
rollback planning.

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS 1. Verify the approved change reference. 2.
Confirm the assets and patch scope. 3. Obtain explicit reboot
authorization. 4. Do not expand the change scope without approval. 5.
Verify patch completion and service health. 6. Record evidence and
approvals for audit. 7. Escalate failures using the Evergreen Financial
Ticket Process.

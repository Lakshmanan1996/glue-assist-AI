CSOP - PP - PATCH PROCESS - WESTBRIDGE PROPERTY GROUP Environment: LAB
SAMPLE ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

OVERVIEW These instructions define patching and reboot schedules for
Westbridge Property Group offices and sites. Building operations and
site-specific maintenance registers must be checked.

SERVER PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Monthly Day: Third Sunday of each month Time: 12:00 AM - 4:00 AM Local
Site Time

Patch Remediation Apply approved patches during the maintenance window.
Confirm the site and time zone for each asset.

Reboot Policy Standard IT infrastructure servers may be rebooted during
the approved window when covered by the maintenance plan. Building
access, CCTV, fire, lift, and life-safety systems are excluded from this
general policy and must not be patched or rebooted by the NOC under this
SOP.

WORKSTATION PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Saturday Time: 11:00 PM - 4:00 AM Local Site Time

Reboot Policy Standard office workstations may be configured for
automated reboot at 3:00 AM Sunday. Reception and site operations
workstations must be coordinated with the local site contact.

PATCHING EXCEPTIONS Building and Life-Safety Systems These systems
require a separate vendor/customer-approved maintenance procedure. A
general IT maintenance window is not authorization to modify or reboot
them.

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS 1. Confirm site, asset, and local time zone. 2.
Check the site maintenance register. 3. Verify the device is not a
building or life-safety system. 4. Patch within the approved window. 5.
Reboot only under the applicable approval. 6. Verify patch results and
service health. 7. Document failures and escalate through the Westbridge
Ticket Process.

CSOP - PP - PATCH PROCESS - NORTHSTAR MANUFACTURING Environment: LAB
SAMPLE ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

OVERVIEW These instructions define approved patching, remediation, and
reboot procedures for managed Northstar Manufacturing devices. Patch
activities must occur only during approved maintenance windows unless an
authorized exception is documented.

SERVER PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Saturday Time: 12:00 AM - 6:00 AM Central Time

Patch Remediation Install approved operating-system and security updates
during the maintenance window. Review failed updates and record the
affected server and error details.

Reboot Policy Production application servers require approval before
reboot. Infrastructure servers may be rebooted during the approved
window when covered by the approved maintenance plan. Do not reboot
manufacturing control or production-line systems under this general
policy.

WORKSTATION PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Saturday Time: 1:00 AM - 5:00 AM Central Time

Reboot Policy Standard office workstations may be configured for
automated reboot at 4:00 AM Saturday. Coordinate exceptions for devices
used by overnight operations.

PATCHING EXCEPTIONS Production Control Systems Do not install patches or
reboot production control systems under the standard schedule. These
devices require a separate approved change and maintenance window from
the customer’s operations team.

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS 1. Confirm the device belongs to the applicable
patch group. 2. Verify the approved maintenance window and time zone. 3.
Confirm patch installation results. 4. Reboot only according to the
applicable device policy and approval. 5. Record failed patches, reboot
failures, and exceptions. 6. Escalate patch-related issues using the
customer’s Ticket Process. 7. Maintain ticket notes for all patching
activities.

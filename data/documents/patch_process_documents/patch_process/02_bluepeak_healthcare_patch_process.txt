CSOP - PP - PATCH PROCESS - BLUEPEAK HEALTHCARE Environment: LAB SAMPLE
ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

OVERVIEW This procedure defines approved patching and reboot
requirements for BluePeak Healthcare managed infrastructure. All
patching must follow the maintenance windows below. Clinical systems
have additional approval requirements.

SERVER PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Monthly Day: Second Sunday of each month Time: 12:00 AM - 4:00 AM
Eastern Time

Patch Remediation Install approved updates during the monthly window.
Validate service health after patching and record any failed updates.

Reboot Policy Infrastructure servers may be rebooted during the approved
window when included in the approved change. Clinical application
servers and identity services require explicit customer approval before
reboot.

WORKSTATION PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Sunday Time: 1:00 AM - 5:00 AM Eastern Time

Reboot Policy Standard administrative workstations may be configured for
automated reboot at 4:00 AM Sunday. Shared clinical workstations must
not be rebooted while in active use. Coordinate with the customer
contact.

PATCHING EXCEPTIONS Clinical Systems Clinical application servers,
patient-care systems, and designated shared clinical workstations are
excluded from automatic patching and reboot under this general policy.
Use a separately approved change window.

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS 1. Confirm the device’s patch classification.
2. Validate the approved window and change record. 3. Do not reboot
clinical systems without explicit approval. 4. Verify patch completion
and service health. 5. Document failed installations and reboot issues.
6. Escalate according to the BluePeak Ticket Process. 7. Do not include
patient information in ticket notes.

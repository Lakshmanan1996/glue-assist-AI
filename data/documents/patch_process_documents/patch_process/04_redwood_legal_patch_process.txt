CSOP - PP - PATCH PROCESS - REDWOOD LEGAL SERVICES Environment: LAB
SAMPLE ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

OVERVIEW This document defines the approved patch and reboot process for
Redwood Legal Services. Production changes must be traceable to an
approved maintenance or change record.

SERVER PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Monthly Day: Third Saturday of each month Time: 11:00 PM - 4:00 AM
Eastern Time

Patch Remediation Install approved operating-system and security updates
within the window. Record update results and any outstanding failures.

Reboot Policy Shared file, document-management, and authentication
servers require explicit approval before reboot. Other infrastructure
servers may be rebooted only when the approved change record includes
the reboot.

WORKSTATION PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Saturday Time: 11:00 PM - 5:00 AM Eastern Time

Reboot Policy Standard workstations may be configured for automated
reboot at 4:30 AM Sunday. Devices assigned to active legal proceedings
or scheduled business-critical work must be coordinated with the user or
customer IT contact.

PATCHING EXCEPTIONS Document Management Services Do not reboot
document-management servers or shared storage outside an approved
change. Confirm the service owner and change reference before
proceeding.

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS 1. Verify the approved change and maintenance
window. 2. Confirm affected assets and service owners. 3. Obtain
required reboot approval. 4. Verify patch status and service
availability. 5. Document failures and all approvals. 6. Escalate
through the Redwood Ticket Process. 7. Do not place confidential client
content in ticket notes.

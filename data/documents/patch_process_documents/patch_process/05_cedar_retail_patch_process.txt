CSOP - PP - PATCH PROCESS - CEDAR RETAIL Environment: LAB SAMPLE ONLY -
FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

OVERVIEW These instructions define patching and reboot schedules for
Cedar Retail stores and head office. Store trading hours and
store-specific maintenance schedules must be considered.

SERVER PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Sunday Time: 12:00 AM - 4:00 AM Local Store Time

Patch Remediation Install approved patches during each store’s
maintenance window. Confirm the store identifier and local time zone
before scheduling.

Reboot Policy Store infrastructure servers may be rebooted only within
the approved store window and when the store is not trading, unless an
approved exception exists. Central retail platform servers require an
approved change and explicit reboot authorization.

WORKSTATION PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Sunday Time: 1:00 AM - 5:00 AM Local Store Time

Reboot Policy Office workstations may be configured for automated reboot
at 4:00 AM Sunday. Point-of-sale terminals and payment devices must not
be rebooted under the general workstation schedule.

PATCHING EXCEPTIONS Point-of-Sale and Payment Devices These devices are
excluded from general automated patching and reboot. Follow the separate
payment-system maintenance procedure and obtain customer approval.

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS 1. Confirm store ID and local time zone. 2.
Check the store trading schedule. 3. Verify device patch group and
approved window. 4. Do not reboot payment devices under this policy. 5.
Confirm patch installation and device health. 6. Document failures and
escalate through the Cedar Retail Ticket Process.

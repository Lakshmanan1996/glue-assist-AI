CSOP - PP - PATCH PROCESS - OCEANVIEW HOSPITALITY Environment: LAB
SAMPLE ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

OVERVIEW This procedure defines patching and reboot schedules for
Oceanview Hospitality properties and central services. Property events
and occupancy-related operational requirements must be checked before
maintenance.

SERVER PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Monthly Day: First Monday of each month Time: 1:00 AM - 5:00 AM Local
Property Time

Patch Remediation Apply approved patches during the property maintenance
window. Confirm property-specific time zone and check the event
calendar.

Reboot Policy Property infrastructure servers may be rebooted only when
the approved window and property schedule permit. Reservation and
guest-service platform servers require explicit customer approval.

WORKSTATION PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Monday Time: 2:00 AM - 5:00 AM Local Property Time

Reboot Policy Back-office workstations may be configured for automated
reboot at 4:00 AM Monday. Front-desk and guest-service workstations must
not be rebooted during active shifts.

PATCHING EXCEPTIONS Major Events The customer may register a property or
device exception during conferences or major events. The exception must
include property, device, approved period, and approver. Do not assume
the exception applies to all properties.

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS 1. Check the property event calendar. 2.
Confirm local property time and device scope. 3. Patch only during the
approved window. 4. Coordinate reboot of front-desk systems. 5. Verify
service health after patching. 6. Document failures and escalate through
the Oceanview Ticket Process.

CSOP - PP - PATCH PROCESS - SUMMIT EDUCATION GROUP Environment: LAB
SAMPLE ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

OVERVIEW These instructions define patching and reboot schedules for
Summit Education Group. The school term calendar must be checked before
carrying out maintenance.

SERVER PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Monthly Day: First Sunday of each month Time: 12:00 AM - 5:00 AM Pacific
Time

Patch Remediation Apply approved patches during the maintenance window.
Confirm server health and application availability after updates.

Reboot Policy Approved infrastructure servers may be rebooted during the
monthly window. School management and learning-platform servers require
approval if the reboot falls during an active school event or
examination period.

WORKSTATION PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY Patch Maintenance Window Frequency:
Weekly Day: Sunday Time: 12:00 AM - 6:00 AM Pacific Time

Reboot Policy Standard staff workstations may be configured for
automated reboot at 5:00 AM Sunday. Classroom and examination-room
devices must follow the school calendar and must not be rebooted during
scheduled examinations or teaching activities.

PATCHING EXCEPTIONS Examination Period Devices listed on the approved
examination exception register must not be rebooted during the
registered period. The exception must include device/site, start date,
end date, and approver.

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS 1. Check the school term and examination
calendar. 2. Confirm the asset is included in the patch group. 3. Patch
only during the approved window. 4. Reboot according to the applicable
device policy. 5. Validate installation and reboot results. 6. Record
failures and escalate using the Ticket Process.

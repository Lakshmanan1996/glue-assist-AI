CUSTOMER SOP - CEDAR RETAIL Document: Store and Head Office Alert
Process Environment: LAB SAMPLE ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

1.  PURPOSE Defines NOC handling for store systems, point-of-sale
    support infrastructure, and head office services.

2.  SEVERITY Critical:

-   Multiple stores unable to process transactions, central retail
    platform outage, or major WAN outage.
-   Begin triage and contact the escalation chain.
-   Do not make changes to payment systems or transaction data.

High: - Single-store server, network, or shared service outage. -
Investigate and determine store impact. - If a store employee must check
cabling or power, set “Escalated to Customer” and explain the required
action.

3.  CONTACT ESCALATION Fictional lab contacts:

4.  Retail Service Desk - +1-555-0501 - retaildesk@cedar-retail.example

5.  Regional IT Manager - +1-555-0502 - regionalit@cedar-retail.example

6.  Head Office Duty Contact - +1-555-0503 - duty@cedar-retail.example
    For Critical multi-store impact, call twice at each level. For a
    single-store issue, contact the service desk first.

7.  AVAILABILITY

-   Transaction platform and multi-store outages: 24x7.
-   Store endpoint issues: store operating hours.
-   Head office user issues: weekdays, 09:00-17:00.

5.  REMOTE REMEDIATION

-   Read-only monitoring is allowed.
-   Store server remediation is allowed only within the approved action
    list.
-   Payment terminals and payment applications must not be remotely
    modified by the NOC.

6.  REBOOT

-   Obtain approval before rebooting a store server during trading
    hours.
-   Outside trading hours, verify the approved maintenance window before
    proceeding.
-   Never reboot payment infrastructure without explicit authorization.

7.  MAINTENANCE EXCEPTIONS

-   Check store-specific maintenance schedule.
-   Confirm store number, device name, time zone, and end time.
-   An exception for one store does not apply to other stores.

8.  TICKET DOCUMENTATION Include store ID, business impact, device,
    timestamps, tests, approvals, customer instructions, and resolution.

CUSTOMER SOP - WESTBRIDGE PROPERTY GROUP Document: Property IT and
Building Systems Alert Process Environment: LAB SAMPLE ONLY - FICTIONAL
CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

1.  PURPOSE Defines NOC handling for office IT and property-site
    infrastructure alerts.

2.  SEVERITY HANDLING Critical:

-   Multiple property sites offline, central network outage, or a
    critical shared business service unavailable.
-   Contact escalation contacts immediately.
-   If a physical site visit or power/cabling check is required, set
    “Escalated to Customer”.

High: - Single property server or network device unavailable. - Confirm
whether the issue affects tenants, staff, or building operations. - If
information is missing, add a public note and set “Info Needed by NOC”.

3.  ESCALATION CONTACTS Fictional lab contacts:

4.  Property IT Service Desk - +1-555-1001 -
    servicedesk@westbridge-property.example

5.  Regional Facilities IT - +1-555-1002 -
    facilitiesit@westbridge-property.example

6.  Corporate Infrastructure Lead - +1-555-1003 -
    infra-lead@westbridge-property.example For Critical incidents, call
    each contact up to two times before moving to the next.

7.  CONTACT AVAILABILITY

-   Central network and multi-site outages: 24x7.
-   Single-site non-critical issues: weekdays, 08:00-17:00.
-   Building access or safety systems must be referred to the customer’s
    designated facilities team.

5.  REMOTE REMEDIATION

-   Monitoring and read-only diagnostics are permitted.
-   Do not modify building access, CCTV, fire, lift, or other
    life-safety systems.
-   Server and network changes require customer authorization.

6.  REBOOT APPROVAL

-   Obtain approval before restarting shared property services.
-   For network devices, confirm site impact and approved change window.
-   If approval is not received, escalate and leave the device
    unchanged.

7.  MAINTENANCE EXCEPTIONS

-   Check the property maintenance register.
-   Confirm site, asset, approver, and expiry date.
-   Do not treat safety-system alerts as covered by a general IT
    maintenance exception.

8.  TICKET DOCUMENTATION Record property/site, affected service,
    business impact, diagnostics, customer approvals, escalation
    attempts, and ticket status.

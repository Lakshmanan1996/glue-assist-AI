CUSTOMER SOP - REDWOOD LEGAL SERVICES Document: IT Alert and Escalation
Procedure Environment: LAB SAMPLE ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

1.  PURPOSE Provides the NOC workflow for infrastructure and endpoint
    alerts.

2.  SEVERITY HANDLING Critical:

-   Firm-wide connectivity outage, file service outage, or
    security-related infrastructure outage.
-   Contact the escalation chain immediately.
-   Preserve relevant logs and evidence.
-   Do not delete files, disable accounts, or alter security controls
    without authorization.

High: - Investigate affected servers and services. - If additional
information is needed, add a public note and set “Info Needed by NOC”. -
If local hands or user action is required, set “Escalated to Customer”.

3.  CONTACT ORDER Fictional lab contacts:

4.  IT Support Desk - +1-555-0401 - support@redwood-legal.example

5.  IT Director - +1-555-0402 - director.it@redwood-legal.example

6.  Managing Partner Delegate - +1-555-0403 -
    delegate@redwood-legal.example Call twice at each level for Critical
    incidents. Use the approved customer contact channel and document
    attempts.

7.  CONTACT AVAILABILITY

-   Critical server or firm-wide incidents: 24x7.
-   User workstation issues: weekdays, 08:30-17:30.
-   Do not contact individual partners directly unless the escalation
    list instructs it.

5.  REMOTE REMEDIATION

-   Monitoring and read-only diagnostics are allowed.
-   Obtain approval before modifying document-management systems, access
    permissions, or security policies.
-   Treat client matter data as confidential; do not copy it into ticket
    notes.

6.  REBOOT POLICY

-   Obtain approval before rebooting shared servers or
    document-management services.
-   For user devices, coordinate with the user.
-   If approval is not received within 15 minutes during a Critical
    incident, escalate; do not infer approval.

7.  MAINTENANCE

-   Validate maintenance windows against the approved change record.
-   Record the change ID and the assets in scope.
-   Alerts outside the approved scope follow normal handling.

8.  DOCUMENTATION Record minimum necessary technical details, evidence,
    approval, actions, contact attempts, and status. Never paste
    confidential client documents into tickets.

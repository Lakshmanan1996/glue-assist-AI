CUSTOMER SOP - BLUEPEAK HEALTHCARE Document: Monitoring Alert and Ticket
Process Environment: LAB SAMPLE ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

1.  PURPOSE This procedure covers NOC handling of infrastructure alerts
    for BluePeak Healthcare.

2.  SEVERITY AND RESPONSE Critical:

-   Critical includes a site outage, core identity service outage, or
    outage affecting clinical systems.
-   Notify the customer escalation contacts immediately.
-   Do not make changes to clinical applications or patient-care systems
    without explicit authorization.
-   If information is missing, add a public note and set “Info Needed by
    NOC”.
-   If onsite or customer action is needed, add a public note and set
    “Escalated to Customer”.

High: - Investigate infrastructure symptoms and collect logs. - Escalate
if the issue affects clinical operations or multiple departments. - For
non-urgent issues, continue during the approved support window.

3.  CONTACT ESCALATION Fictional lab contacts:

4.  IT Service Desk - +1-555-0201 - servicedesk@bluepeak.example

5.  Infrastructure Lead - +1-555-0202 - infra@bluepeak.example

6.  On-call Administrator - +1-555-0203 - oncall@bluepeak.example For
    Critical incidents, call twice at each level before moving to the
    next. Document timestamps and outcomes.

7.  AVAILABILITY

-   Critical infrastructure incidents: 24x7.
-   Non-critical workstation incidents: Monday-Friday, 08:00-18:00
    customer local time.
-   Contact clinical department staff only through the designated IT
    contact.

5.  REMOTE REMEDIATION

-   Read-only diagnostics and monitoring are permitted 24x7.
-   Changes to clinical systems, identity services, or shared clinical
    workstations require approval.
-   Do not interrupt active clinical sessions.

6.  REBOOT POLICY

-   Reboots of clinical application servers require explicit approval.
-   For other servers, document impact and request approval according to
    the change process.
-   Wait up to 20 minutes for a response, then escalate to the next
    contact. No automatic reboot is authorized.

7.  MAINTENANCE

-   Planned maintenance must be confirmed against the customer change
    calendar.
-   Record the change reference and approved start/end times.
-   An alert during an approved window must still be checked against the
    maintenance scope.

8.  DOCUMENTATION Record diagnostic evidence, affected service, customer
    approval, communication, escalation, and outcome. Do not include
    patient information in ticket notes.

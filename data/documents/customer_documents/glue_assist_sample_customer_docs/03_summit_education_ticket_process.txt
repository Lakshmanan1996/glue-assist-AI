CUSTOMER SOP - SUMMIT EDUCATION GROUP Document: Datto RMM Ticket
Handling Environment: LAB SAMPLE ONLY - FICTIONAL CUSTOMER Version: 1.0

1.  PURPOSE Defines the NOC process for school and administrative IT
    alerts.

2.  ALERT CLASSIFICATION Critical:

-   Multiple school sites offline, core network outage, or
    authentication outage affecting broad access.
-   Notify the escalation contacts immediately.
-   If customer action is required, set status to “Escalated to
    Customer” with a clear public note.

High: - A single site server or important shared service is degraded. -
Investigate and update the ticket. - If blocked by missing details, set
status to “Info Needed by NOC”.

Medium/Low: - Queue for normal business-hours handling unless impact
increases.

3.  ESCALATION CONTACTS Fictional lab contacts:

4.  Helpdesk Coordinator - +1-555-0301 - helpdesk@summit-edu.example

5.  IT Operations Manager - +1-555-0302 - itops@summit-edu.example

6.  District Technology Lead - +1-555-0303 - techlead@summit-edu.example
    For Critical alerts, call each contact up to two times before moving
    to the next.

7.  CONTACT HOURS

-   Critical multi-site outages: 24x7 during term time.
-   School workstation and classroom device issues: weekdays,
    07:00-17:00.
-   During school holidays, follow the holiday support calendar.

5.  REMOTE ACTIONS

-   Monitoring and read-only checks are permitted.
-   Server changes require approval from IT Operations.
-   Do not remotely restart classroom devices during teaching hours
    without approval.

6.  REBOOT APPROVAL

-   Shared servers: approval required.
-   Individual staff workstation: coordinate with the user and confirm
    work is saved.
-   Wait 30 minutes for a response to a non-critical approval request;
    escalate Critical incidents immediately.

7.  MAINTENANCE EXCEPTIONS

-   Check the term/holiday maintenance calendar.
-   Record the affected site, asset, approved window, and change
    reference.
-   Do not assume an entire site is under maintenance because one device
    is listed.

8.  TICKET NOTES Record site, asset, impact, tests, user coordination,
    approval, and resolution. Avoid storing student personal information
    in ticket notes.
